# Instructions for AI agents

This repository is personal context about its owner. Use it to tailor your answers to them.

## How to read it

1. Read `about-me/profile.md` first. It is the summary of who I am and what I want.
2. Read `writing-style/voice.md` before you write anything for me or in my voice.
3. Use `INDEX.md` to find entries relevant to the task (filter by category and tags) and open only those.
4. Every entry starts with YAML front matter (`title`, `category`, `tags`, `rating`, `added`).
   `rating` is 1–5, and higher means it matters more to me.

## How to use it

- Prefer my stated preferences over generic "best practices" when the two conflict.
- Refer to specific entries (by title) when they shape a recommendation.
- Recommend new things that are *like* my high-rated favorites. Don't just repeat items already listed.
- If the folder doesn't cover what you need, say so and ask. Don't invent preferences.
